Project Overview

The BAJA SAE project involves the multidisciplinary design and engineering of an off-road competition vehicle as part of the Borregos Baja Racing team at Tecnológico de Monterrey. The vehicle was developed as an integrated mechanical system, requiring simultaneous consideration of the powertrain, chassis, front and rear suspension, steering, and braking systems. As team captain, I led this comprehensive engineering workflow from mathematical modeling and CAD design through simulation, CAE/FEA analysis, optimization, component selection, testing, and subsystem integration. Engineering decisions were driven by vehicle performance, structural integrity, packaging, weight distribution, manufacturability, and off-road operating conditions.

Engineering Disciplines & Core Competencies

  • Powertrain Modeling & Vehicle Dynamics: Physics-based MATLAB/Simulink simulation incorporating traction force, aerodynamic drag, rolling resistance, gradient resistance, CVT transmission behavior, and tire characteristics. Model-based methodology for predicting vehicle acceleration and velocity throughout operating range with experimental validation.
  • CVT Tuning & Data-Driven Optimization: Systematic evaluation of CVT mechanical parameters (drive clutch spring, ramp geometry, flyweight mass, driven spring, CAM geometry) using multi-parameter optimization and weighted scoring methodology. Evaluation across acceleration, hill climb, pulling capability, endurance, and maneuverability performance.
  • Drivetrain Design & Structural Validation: Specification of Gaged Engineering CVT with 2.5:1 gearbox, 3.5:1 final-drive reduction, CVT ratios 3.5:1 to 1.1:1, and calculated 522 Nm torque capability. Integration of experimental SuperFlow test system measurements with FEA validation for structural components using aluminum 6061-T6 and AISI 4130 steel.
  • Lightweight Tubular Chassis Design: AISI 4130 steel space-frame architecture with optimized tube diameters and wall thicknesses. Achieved ~31 kg frame mass, 49/51 weight distribution, 2847 Nm/rad torsional stiffness. Primary, secondary, and tertiary structural members differentiated by mechanical function through CAD modeling and FEA.
  • Suspension Kinematics & Analysis: Unequal-length double-wishbone front suspension with 234 mm wheel travel, rear trailing-arm architecture with 178 mm travel. Kinematic evaluation including camber-versus-displacement, toe-versus-displacement, and roll-center positioning analysis with Fox Float dampers. Spring-rate selection and 1.9 Hz sprung-mass natural frequency optimization.
  • Steering Geometry & Mechanical Integration: Rack-and-pinion steering with 22° Ackermann angle, 20° caster, 10° kingpin inclination, 93.8 mm kinematic trail. FEA validation of critical components with maximum stress 106.66 MPa. Integration of steering kinematics with suspension geometry and tire-ground interaction through CAD and structural analysis.
  • Hydraulic Braking System Engineering: Disc-brake architecture with CNC aluminum master cylinders, motorcycle dual-piston calipers, and custom 1018 steel rotors. Engineering calculations relating wheel braking force, brake torque, stopping distance, deceleration, pad friction coefficient, and safety-critical design requirements.
  • Driver Ergonomics & Digital Human Modeling: Porter Comfort Study integrated into CAD workflow with 95th-percentile human model accommodation. Optimization of driver seating, steering-wheel location, pedal reach, and roll-cage geometry. Human-centered approach ensuring visibility, control accessibility, and center-of-gravity management.
  • Systems Integration & Iterative Design: Interface management across suspension hardpoints, steering geometry, drivetrain packaging, brake integration, and ergonomic constraints. Applied genuine systems-engineering methodology combining analytical calculations, CAD assemblies, CAE/FEA, vehicle-dynamics modeling, MATLAB/Simulink simulation, experimental validation, and physical subsystem integration.
  • Team Leadership & Multidisciplinary Coordination: Captain of 42-person engineering team across mechanical, drivetrain, suspension, steering, braking, and systems disciplines. Strategic coordination of design, analysis, manufacturing, testing, and competition deployment phases.

Integrated Engineering Outcomes

The Baja SAE project demonstrates professional-level automotive engineering experience spanning CAD design, CAE/FEA structural analysis, MATLAB/Simulink simulation, vehicle dynamics modeling, mechanical design optimization, CVT calibration, experimental drivetrain characterization, material selection, digital human modeling, and systems integration. The resulting vehicle architecture incorporated a lightweight tubular chassis, continuously variable transmission, custom drivetrain, long-travel suspension, rack-and-pinion steering, and hydraulic disc braking—each subsystem developed with rigorous analysis and validated through simulation and testing. This integrated approach demonstrates capability in automotive engineering, motorsport systems, mechanical design, simulation-based validation, vehicle dynamics, and model-based engineering under real competition constraints.

Development Process & Engineering Workflow

1

Systems Concept & Requirements Analysis

Define integrated vehicle architecture based on competition rules, performance targets, off-road operating conditions, and competition constraints. Mathematical modeling of powertrain, vehicle dynamics, and subsystem requirements.

2

Detailed Design, Modeling & Simulation

Complete CAD assemblies for all systems. Physics-based MATLAB/Simulink vehicle dynamics modeling with experimental validation. CAE/FEA structural analysis for chassis, suspension, steering, and drivetrain components. Kinematic analysis of suspension and steering geometry. CVT parameter evaluation and multi-parameter optimization.

3

Component Optimization & Integration

Material selection and thickness optimization for structural components. Integration of suspension hardpoints, steering geometry, and drivetrain packaging through iterative CAD refinement. Digital human modeling for driver ergonomics. SuperFlow test system characterization of drivetrain performance. FEA validation of maximum stresses against material yield strengths.

4

Manufacturing, Assembly & Field Validation

Execute CAM operations and coordinate precision manufacturing of custom chassis tubes, drivetrain components, steering parts, and braking system. Assembly integration following engineering drawings and CAD specifications. Comprehensive field testing including suspension travel validation, drivetrain performance measurement, steering response evaluation, and off-road durability assessment before competition deployment.

5

Competition Deployment & Continuous Improvement

Vehicle testing and optimization at competition events. Data-driven analysis of performance results. Iterative design improvements across multiple vehicle generations, with lessons learned from field performance integrated into subsequent design cycles.
